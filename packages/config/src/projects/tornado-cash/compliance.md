### Enforcement

A relayer can refuse to submit a withdrawal. The user then picks another or submits it from their own wallet. Circle and Tether can freeze the USDC and USDT that the token pools hold. OFAC's listing and delisting of the pools are under [milestones](#milestones-and-incidents).

### Voluntary disclosure

0xbow's [Proof of Association](https://tornado.0xbow.io), an outside tool, proves that a withdrawal's deposit is on 0xbow's list, without showing which deposit. It posts the proof to a registry contract, where it also narrows the withdrawal's possible deposits to that list. 0xbow curates the list and says it excludes deposits tied to sanctioned or criminal groups, hacks, theft and scams.
