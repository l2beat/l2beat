The {{multisigStats}} Privacy Pools Multisig holds the Entrypoint's owner role. It can upgrade the Entrypoint, register, configure and wind down pools, set fees and withdraw the vetting fees, all without delay. It also appoints the ASP postmen, who post the approved roots. The pools and verifiers are immutable. Relayed withdrawals and new deposits pass through the Entrypoint, so a malicious upgrade can steal them. Ragequit works without the Entrypoint, so funds already in the pools can always leave to their depositing addresses.
