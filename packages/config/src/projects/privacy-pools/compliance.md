### Enforcement

0xbow may ask a depositor for a Proof of Association, which its [separate app](https://tornado.0xbow.io) creates for funds that left Tornado Cash. Each pool accepts a private withdrawal only with a proof against the latest approved list. The proof is public: a recipient sees 0xbow's approval, and nothing beyond it, without asking the user. The approved list is also the set a withdrawal hides in, which 0xbow can shrink. The contracts accept no second list and check no withdrawal address. The approved lists are public on IPFS.
