Ethereum uses proof-of-stake with 12-second slots. In each slot, one validator index is pseudo-randomly selected to propose a block, with selection probability proportional to effective stake. The proposer can construct and order the execution payload locally or outsource construction to specialized builders through off-protocol software such as MEV-Boost. With outsourced construction, a builder chooses the transactions and their ordering inside a candidate payload, while the proposer chooses among the bids it receives and signs the winning block.

Validators attest to blocks and apply fork choice, but the attestation committees do not co-produce or pre-approve the transactions in a block. Block-building rights are permissionless: anyone can deposit at least 32 ETH and enter the activation queue, subject to the protocol's effective-balance churn limit.
