### Enforcement

Cloaked checks the user's IP address for sanctions and geographic limits, as its [privacy policy](https://clkd.xyz/docs/privacy) says. Its [terms](https://clkd.xyz/docs/terms) bar users under sanctions and let it suspend an account. Tokenized stocks carry their issuers' country limits. Incoming payments land at addresses that check nothing. The app and its servers are closed, so the checks show only in what Cloaked publishes.

### Data access

The policy lets Cloaked answer legal requests. The IP check runs on every session. The policy says the address is not stored for long.
