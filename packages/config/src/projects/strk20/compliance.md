### Enforcement

The pool accepts a deposit from a Starknet address only with a [signature](https://github.com/starkware-libs/starknet-privacy/blob/fcc7c90f544ceb08621c32616bde29f1fdcbeb54/packages/privacy/src/privacy.cairo#L784-L803) of its screener key, at most 300 seconds old. The [screening service](https://github.com/starkware-libs/starknet-privacy/blob/f7fb72a9a7d0cb1a2ad447fec55c1207029ae093/elliptic-proxy/src/handler.ts#L185-L273) refuses addresses [exposed](https://github.com/starkware-libs/starknet-privacy/blob/f7fb72a9a7d0cb1a2ad447fec55c1207029ae093/elliptic-proxy/src/scoring.ts#L40-L99) to illicit activity, sanctioned entities, obfuscation, the Bybit exploit or terrorism above set amounts. Its own lists override Elliptic. Only provers with partner credentials get a signature. Five helper contracts are exempt, among them the CCTP bridge, so USDC bridged in from other chains enters unscreened. Transfers and withdrawals are not checked: a refused address can still register, receive from others and withdraw. The governance multisig can [replace](#upgrades-and-governance) the screener and auditor keys at once.
