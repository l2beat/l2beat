### Enforcement

The check [runs in the app's SDK](https://github.com/ScopeLift/umbra-protocol/blob/53ed4af5e575919680a4e70c1a090feafe365275/umbra-js/src/utils/utils.ts#L1100-L1190) when a wallet connects, pays, withdraws or scans. The app also hides payments from or to listed addresses. The app [sends token withdrawals](https://github.com/ScopeLift/umbra-protocol/blob/53ed4af5e575919680a4e70c1a090feafe365275/frontend/src/components/AccountReceiveTable.vue#L796-L820) only through ScopeLift's closed relayer, which can refuse one. Whether the relayer screens is not known. ScopeLift's list sits in the SDK's source, and the oracle is an onchain contract. Chainalysis alone decides what its oracle lists.
