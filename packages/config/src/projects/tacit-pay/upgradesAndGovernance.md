The TacitEvmPool, its router, zap and verifier are immutable and have no owner, pause or admin function. A new circuit or rule needs a new pool at a new address, and users move by withdrawing and depositing again.

The anon.wei app is a chain of immutable page contracts. The Z DAO appends new versions and owns the name anon.wei, which it can point at any contract. It also owns two registries the app reads every six hours. Through these it can add RPC nodes, replace the relay host and add proving-key mirrors. An RPC node or relay the user sets in the app takes precedence.

Holders of Z Shares vote in the Z DAO, the Moloch contract. A proposal passes once 10% of the shares have voted with more votes for than against, and can execute 1 day after it is queued. The app's previous owner holds about a fifth of the votes and can pass a proposal alone unless more votes oppose it within a day. Anyone can mint shares one for one with ZAMM, a token with a fixed supply.
