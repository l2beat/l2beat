The vault, verifier, signing keys and exit parameters are immutable. The owner EOA can set the treasury, pause deposits, cooperative closes and the start of escapes, and transfer or renounce ownership, all without delay. Pending escapes still finalize during a pause. An instant pause with a subsequent sweep after expiry would steal all funds whose escape was not started before the pause.

The operator's services, the hosted app and the daemon releases are controlled by Open Anonymity and can change without notice.
