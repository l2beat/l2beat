## Funds can be stolen if
1. the zk proof system is broken, allowing invalid spends or withdrawals.
2. all [trusted setup](#trusted-setups) participants collude or leak their secrets, allowing forged proofs.
3. the DAO passes a malicious [upgrade](#upgrades-and-governance) and users stay in the pool past its {{executionDelay}} execution delay.
<br>
## Funds can be lost if
1. a user loses the keys of their private balance.
<br>
## Privacy can be lost if
1. a user sends transactions from their own public address (see public observer).
