### Enforcement

The user's Safes check no list, and payments into them need no approval. Fluidkey's [terms](https://www.fluidkey.com/terms) bar users under sanctions and let it suspend an account. Bank transfers run through Noah and need [identity verification](https://docs.fluidkey.com/guides/how-to-complete-kyc-on-fluidkey) with Sumsub and Noah. [Hide Trail](https://docs.fluidkey.com/readme/advanced-privacy), an optional route for more privacy, sends funds through Houdini and two exchanges that monitor for AML. Those exchanges see the route and can hold funds in transit. The app and its servers are closed, so the checks show only in what Fluidkey publishes.

### Data access

Fluidkey's [policy](https://www.fluidkey.com/privacy) lets it disclose data to meet a legal duty. A bank user is known to Noah by name.
