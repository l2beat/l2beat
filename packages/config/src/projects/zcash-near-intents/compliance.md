### Enforcement

The operator, registered in the British Virgin Islands, names its [vendors](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/risk-and-compliance): Binance AML, AMLBot, PureFi and TRM Labs. It checks each deposit again before crediting it. Its [terms](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/terms-of-service) let it delay, block or freeze bridged funds. Market makers pass KYB, end users swap without an account. Zcash's shielded pool, between the two swaps, checks nothing. The screening service is closed.

### Access to private data

The listed vendors can receive each quote's addresses, before the swap exists. The terms let the operator collect IP and wallet addresses. It takes law enforcement requests through a portal.

### Proof of clean origin

Both swaps are public on NEAR, and the [viewing key](https://github.com/chainapsis/vizor-wallet/blob/43c1b3467fc00c630fcd7eb8e8070b8b4d409596/lib/src/features/settings/screens/settings_viewing_key_screen.dart#L25-L30) connects them across the shielded pool. Vizor also exports an incoming-only key.
