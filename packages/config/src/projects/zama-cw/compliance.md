### Enforcement

The five tokens with an issuer denylist are cUSDC, cUSDT, cXAUT, cTGBP and cAUSD. The owner, a Zama [governance multisig](#upgrades-and-governance), adds to the block list without delay. The ACL's owner can also deny a token's own address, which halts that token for everyone. The app sends the connected address to Elliptic before each transaction and checks the IP's country for some products, as Zama's [privacy notice](https://www.zama.org/legal/zama-app-privacy-notice) says. A direct contract call skips the app's checks and still meets the token's.
