The Hinkal contract holds all shielded funds and cannot be upgraded. A {{multisigStats}} Safe acts through a TimelockController with a {{timelockDelay}} delay. It can replace the verifiers, the HinkalHelper, which builds the public inputs of every proof, the external actions and the relayer list. A malicious verifier or helper can drain the pool or reject every transaction. The same timelock upgrades the Emporium, which can then move the funds of every public account delegated to the HinkalWallet.

One of the Safe's signers controls the HinkalWrapper, the LI.FI action, the DepositOnChainUtxos action and the ReceiveVaultFactory without delay. It can redirect deposits made through the wrapper and cut a swap's output to the minimum the user signed. It can also upgrade the factory, which moves the funds waiting at deposit addresses. Through the DepositOnChainUtxos action it can spend the token approvals that scheduled sends give it.
