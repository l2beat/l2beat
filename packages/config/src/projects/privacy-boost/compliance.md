### Enforcement

Sunnyside Labs' [SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@sunnyside-io/privacy-boost/v/1.3.3) marks each deposit as pending, legit or illicit before it becomes spendable. A transfer can also fail a compliance check. Its [terms](https://www.privacyboost.io/terms) name sanctions screening and geolocation controls. No other relay or prover exists, so nothing inside the pool avoids the check.
