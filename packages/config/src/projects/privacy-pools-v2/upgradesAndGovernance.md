A {{adminThreshold}} multisig holds the default admin, ops admin, upgrader, pauser, unpauser and entrypoint manager roles in the AccessRouter. It can upgrade the PoolVault, Entrypoint, Keystore and ASPRegistry and replace the verifiers. It can pause deposits and spends, which leaves ragequit open, or let the state tree fill by lowering its capacity, which stops ragequit too. It enables assets, sets a vetting fee of up to 99.99% on deposits and caps relayer fees. It also administers the three yield shares, with a fee of at most {{maxYieldFee}} of the yield. It owns RelaySwaps and curates its swap routers and zaps. An EOA holds the postman role that publishes approved-label roots. None of these roles has a delay, so every change applies at once.

The shares hold Aave v3 deposits, whose contracts [Aave governance](https://app.aave.com/governance/) can upgrade after a public vote and delay. Aave's guardian multisig can instantly freeze a reserve, which blocks the app's deposits into its shares, or pause it, which also blocks redeeming them.
