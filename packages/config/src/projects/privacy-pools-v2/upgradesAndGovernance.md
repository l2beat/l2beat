A {{adminThreshold}} multisig holds the default admin, ops admin, upgrader, pauser, unpauser and entrypoint manager roles in the AccessRouter. It can upgrade the PoolVault, Entrypoint, Keystore and ASPRegistry and replace the verifiers. It can pause deposits and spends, which leaves ragequit open, or let the state tree fill by lowering its capacity, which stops ragequit too. It enables assets, sets a vetting fee of up to 99.99% on deposits and caps relayer fees. It also administers the three yield shares, with a fee of at most {{maxYieldFee}} of the yield. The shares hold Aave v3 deposits, whose contracts [Aave governance](https://app.aave.com/governance/) can upgrade after a public vote and delay. Aave's guardian multisig can pause a reserve instantly, which blocks redeeming its shares. An EOA holds the postman role that publishes approved-label roots. Another owns RelaySwaps and curates its routers and zaps. No role has a delay, so every change applies at once.
