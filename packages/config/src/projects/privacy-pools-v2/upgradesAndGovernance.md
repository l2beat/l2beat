One EOA holds the default admin, ops admin, upgrader, pauser, unpauser and entrypoint manager roles in the AccessRouter. It can upgrade the PoolVault, Entrypoint, Keystore and ASPRegistry, replace the verifiers, pause deposits and spends, enable assets and set fee caps, all without delay. It also administers the three yield shares, with a fee of at most {{maxYieldFee}} of the yield. The shares hold Aave v3 deposits, whose contracts Aave governance can upgrade after a public vote and delay. Aave's guardian multisig can pause a reserve instantly, which blocks redeeming its shares. A second EOA holds the postman role that publishes approved-label roots. A third owns RelaySwaps and curates its routers. No timelock or multisig is deployed. Ragequit cannot be paused, so funds already in the vault can leave to their owners as long as the implementation is not changed.
