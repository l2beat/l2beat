### Enforcement

0xbow's [docs](https://docs.privacypools.com/protocol/asp/what-is-an-asp) name sanctions screening, blocklist checks and AML rules. 0xbow approves a deposit, rejects it or asks the depositor to prove the funds' origin first. The PoolVault accepts a private spend only with a proof against the latest approved list. 0xbow's [terms](https://github.com/0xbow-io/privacy-pools-website-v2/blob/5dc5434b443b10aa33c7db5421824d84b71a08b4/config/legal/terms-of-use.ts#L201) let it deny, pause or withdraw an approval at any time. The proof is public: a recipient sees 0xbow's approval, and nothing beyond it, without asking the user. The contracts accept no second list and check no recipient or withdrawal address. The approved lists are public on IPFS. 0xbow also publishes a hash of each label it rejected, without a reason.
