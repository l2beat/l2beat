All privileged functions are ultimately controlled by the PayyMultisig ({{multisigStats}}). It owns the ProxyAdmin, which can instantly upgrade the RollupV1 proxy, and it is the owner of the RollupV1 contract, allowing it to manage provers, validators, ZK verifiers, supported tokens and burn substitutors, and to directly overwrite the state root via setRoot(). There is no timelock or exit window on any of these actions.
