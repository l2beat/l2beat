Taiko uses a multi-proof system to validate state transitions. Exactly two proofs are required from four available verifiers: SGX (Geth), SGX (Reth), SP1, and RISC0. At least one proof must come from SP1 or RISC0. The RISC0 verifier also accepts Groth16 proofs under a verifying key that matches no published RISC Zero trusted setup. While the whitelist is non-empty, non-whitelisted actors cannot submit proofs, and the configured permissionless proving delay is not used to open proving.
