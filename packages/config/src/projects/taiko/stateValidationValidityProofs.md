Taiko uses a multi-proof system to validate state transitions. Exactly two proofs are required from four available verifiers: SGX (Geth), SGX (Reth), SP1, and RISC0. At least one proof must come from SP1 or RISC0: accepted combinations are either SGX verifier plus either ZK verifier, or SP1 plus RISC0. An SGX-only pair is not accepted. New proposals target a proof submission cadence of {{provingWindow}}. Proving is currently centralized behind ProverWhitelist with {{whitelistedProverCount}} whitelisted prover{{proverPlural}}. While the whitelist is non-empty, non-whitelisted actors cannot submit proofs, and the configured permissionless proving delay is not used to open proving.
