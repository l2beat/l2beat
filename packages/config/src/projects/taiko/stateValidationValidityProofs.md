Taiko uses a multi-proof system to validate state transitions. Exactly two proofs are required from four available verifiers: SGX (Geth), SGX (Reth), SP1, and RISC0. At least one proof must come from SP1 or RISC0. RISC Zero has stopped every RISC0 Groth16 verifier except v3.1.0. New proposals target a proof submission cadence of {{provingWindow}}. While the whitelist is non-empty, non-whitelisted actors cannot submit proofs, and the configured permissionless proving delay is not used to open proving.
