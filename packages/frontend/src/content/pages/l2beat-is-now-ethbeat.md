L2BEAT is now ETHBEAT. Same team, same research, same independence, under a name that fits what we cover today: all of Ethereum, not only its layer 2s.

## Why a new name

We started in 2021 to track a handful of rollups, and the name said exactly that. Since then we have added interoperability protocols, privacy protocols, ZK proving systems, DeFi and Ethereum itself. "L2" no longer described the work, and it suggested we look at only one part of the ecosystem. ETHBEAT keeps the beat and names the whole of Ethereum.

## What changed

- **The name and the logo.** ETHBEAT, with a new hand-lettered heart.
- **The address.** The site now lives at **new-domain.tbd**. Every l2beat.com link redirects permanently to the same page there.
- **Link previews.** Pages shared on X, Telegram and elsewhere show new preview images. Platforms cache previews, so older posts may show the old ones for a while.

## What did not change

- **The team and our independence.** The same people do the research, funded in the open as a public good. See [how we are funded](/donate).
- **The methodology.** Our risk frameworks, the [Stages framework](/stages) and every assessment are unchanged. No project was re-scored because of the rename.
- **The data.** Every project page, chart and history is where it was, under the same path.
- **The API.** Same endpoints, same responses. Requests to l2beat.com keep working through the redirect, but point your clients at the new domain when you can: most HTTP clients follow redirects, some do not.
- **Badges.** CROPS badges keep loading from their old URLs and now show the new name. A badge pasted as inline markup keeps its old text until you copy the markup again.
- **Open source.** The code and every project config stay at [github.com/l2beat/l2beat](https://github.com/l2beat/l2beat).
