title: "Sticky table headers and remembered columns"
summary: "Tables keep their headers in view and remember which columns you hid."
publishedAt: "2026-10-02T12:00:00.000Z"
---

Table headers now stay in view while you scroll long tables. Columns you hide are remembered across visits, and the column picker shows how many are hidden. Tables with change columns can also be sorted by the change.
